# Background music

`lounge-1.mp3` comes from [Pixabay](https://pixabay.com/music/), which licenses its audio
for use without attribution, including commercially. It was downsampled to mono 96kbps to
keep the repository small — it is ambience under a voice, not something anyone listens to
on its own.

Dropping other files in here and pointing `LiveRoom.vue` at them works, but anything
committed to this repository has to be licensed for redistribution. Commercial tracks are
not, however common they are in real streams.
